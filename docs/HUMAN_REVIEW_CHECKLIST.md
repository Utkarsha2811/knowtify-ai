# Human Review Checklist for AI-Generated Study Content

Use this checklist before sharing generated output with learners.

## 1) Source fidelity

- [ ] Every key point is present in the uploaded source.
- [ ] No unsupported claim appears in summary/flashcards/quiz.
- [ ] Terminology matches the original material.

## 2) Clarity and writing quality

- [ ] Sentences are concise and easy to read.
- [ ] One idea per bullet where possible.
- [ ] No ambiguous pronouns or vague references.

## 3) Quiz quality

- [ ] Questions are answerable from source context.
- [ ] Options are distinct and not trivial.
- [ ] Correct answer is unambiguous.

## 4) Safety and privacy

- [ ] No sensitive personal/customer data is present.
- [ ] Content is suitable for classroom/study use.

## 5) Final pass

- [ ] Fix factual errors manually.
- [ ] Rewrite awkward lines for readability.
- [ ] Keep the reviewed version as the final reference copy.

---

This checklist keeps a **human accuracy gate** in place and aligns with responsible AI writing workflows.
